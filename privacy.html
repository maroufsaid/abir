Abir: Perfume Collection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Your fragrance collection is personal. This page explains what Abir stores, which Apple services it uses, and the choices you have.

Who operates Abir

Abir: Perfume Collection is operated by Said Marouf (creator name: saidscents). For privacy questions or requests, contact saidscents@gmail.com.

Your collection and wear history

Abir stores the fragrances you add, custom fragrance details, favourites, wear dates and the weather context attached to logged wears. This information powers your collection, recommendations and insights. Recommendations and fragrance-label matching run on your device using the bundled catalogue. No Abir account is required.

Your collection and wear history are stored on your device. When iCloud is available and enabled for the app, they can sync between your devices through your private Apple iCloud database. We do not operate a server that receives your collection or wear history, and we cannot browse your private iCloud database. Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Location and weather

If you allow location access, Abir uses a location fix to request weather from Apple WeatherKit and a place name from Apple’s mapping services. The location can include latitude and longitude, depending on your iOS permission settings. You can also search for and select a city manually; Apple’s mapping services process that search and WeatherKit receives the selected location.

Abir saves the most recent successful location and the selected manual city on your device, along with cached weather. A saved location may be reused for later weather refreshes, including background refreshes, without taking a new location fix. Abir does not continuously track your movement. A logged wear can retain its city name and weather conditions in wear history and iCloud; it does not store GPS coordinates in the wear-log entry.

You can change location permissions in iOS Settings. When Abir next detects that location access has been denied, it clears the automatically cached location. A city you selected manually remains until you clear it in Abir’s Location settings. Revoking permission does not delete city names already attached to past wears. Without live weather, the app can use seasonal estimates.

Camera and scanning

The optional scanner uses the camera to read text on fragrance bottles or boxes. You can also choose a photo through the iOS photo picker; Abir receives only the image you select, rather than unrestricted access to your photo library. Camera images, selected photos and recognised text are processed on your device; Abir does not upload scan images to a recognition service or keep a photo archive of your scans. The fragrance details you choose to add become part of your collection and can sync with iCloud.

Widgets, reminders and diagnostics

Abir shares app data and cached recommendations with its own widget through storage on your device. Pick alerts and streak reminders are scheduled as local iOS notifications. You can control them in the app and in iOS Settings.

The app writes technical diagnostic messages to the device’s system logs. These can include weather context, location details and error information. Abir does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device settings and its privacy policy.

Advertising, sharing and external services

Abir does not include advertising or tracking SDKs, sell your personal data, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Opening an external website or choosing a destination in the iOS share sheet is your choice. The destination service handles the information you send under its own privacy policy. Abir does not automatically send your collection or wear history to social platforms.

Apple provides iCloud, location, mapping and weather services. If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed by our email provider, Gmail, so we can respond. Please avoid sending information you do not want included in your support request.

This support and privacy website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We add no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or embedded social content to these pages. These providers may process information in countries other than where you live, under their own policies and applicable safeguards.

Retention and your choices

Collection and wear records remain until you delete them. You can remove wear entries from History and fragrances from Collection. Removing a fragrance can leave its wear history intact unless you also choose to remove that history. Deletions can sync through iCloud when sync is available. Removing the app does not automatically erase copies stored in iCloud or backups; manage those through your Apple account and iOS storage settings.

The automatically cached location is replaced by a newer successful location or cleared when the app detects denied location access. The manual city remains until cleared. Other local settings and caches remain until replaced, cleared by the app or removed with app data. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address your request and any applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, object to processing, or withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us at the email above. Because we cannot access your private iCloud collection, managing that data may require using the app or your Apple account.

Why information is processed

We process information to provide the features you request, respond to support requests, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required, you can withdraw it; you can also revoke optional device permissions in iOS Settings. The legal basis depends on the processing and applicable law, including performing the service you request, consent where required, and legitimate interests in providing support and protecting the service.

Changes and provider policies

We will update this page when Abir’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. You can also read Apple’s Privacy Policy, GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Google’s Privacy Policy.
